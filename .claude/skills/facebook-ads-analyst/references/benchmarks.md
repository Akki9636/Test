# Meta Ads benchmarks (use as defaults when the user has no targets)

These are broad cross-industry medians. Always prefer the user's own targets,
historicals, or breakeven economics over these. State clearly in the report when
a benchmark (rather than a user target) was used.

## Funnel benchmarks by objective

| Metric | Conversions/Sales | Lead Gen | Traffic | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CTR (link) | 0.9–1.6% | 0.8–1.2% | 1.0–1.5% | < 0.7% = creative/audience gap |
| CPM | varies by market & audience size | — | — | Judge by trend & relative comparison, not absolute |
| Connect rate (LPV/click) | > 80% | > 80% | > 85% | < 70% = slow landing page or broken link |
| CVR (purchase / link click) | 1.5–4% (ecom) | 8–12% (lead form) | — | < 1% ecom = landing page/offer gap |
| Frequency (7-day) | < 2.5 prospecting, < 4 retargeting | same | same | Rising frequency + falling CTR = fatigue |
| Hook rate (3s plays/impr) | > 25% | > 25% | > 25% | < 20% = weak opening |
| Hold rate (ThruPlay/3s) | > 10% | > 10% | > 10% | |

## Vertical CVR adjustments (ecommerce purchase CVR from link clicks)

- Low AOV impulse products (< $50): 2.5–5%
- Mid AOV ($50–$200): 1.5–3%
- High AOV / considered purchase (> $200): 0.5–1.5%
- B2B lead gen (form fill): 5–10% of clicks

## Health heuristics

- **Spend concentration**: if > 70% of spend produces < 30% of results, structural
  reallocation gap.
- **Learning phase**: an ad set needs ~50 optimization events in 7 days to exit
  learning. Below that, expect ±30–50% CPA volatility — don't judge harshly.
- **Statistical caution**: differences in CPA on < 20 conversions per side are
  usually noise. Say so instead of declaring a winner.
- **Breakeven math**: breakeven ROAS = 1 / gross margin. Always compute and show
  it when margin or COGS is known.
